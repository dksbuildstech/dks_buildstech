Hello, I'm Dheer Kumar Sharma. My journey began with the magical piano keys and the golden voice of legends. While creating content that touches millions with Kishore Kumar's immortal songs, I'm simultaneously building my future in technology.

The Music Within

I breathe life into old Bollywood classics through piano. My channel is a tribute to the legends — especially the versatile genius Kishore Kumar. From soulful jukeboxes to relaxing instrumentals, every note is an offering.

The Builder in Me

Through @dksbuildstech I explore electronics, coding, and creative tech projects. Currently immersing myself in Python for data, automation, and AI. HTML, CSS & JavaScript are opening new creative doors.

This website itself is a living demonstration of what I'm learning — parallax scrolling, GSAP animations, interactive elements, and thoughtful design. Every transition represents my belief that technology should feel as magical as music.
